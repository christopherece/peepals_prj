Peepals Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Peepals helps you find a toilet nearby and lets members share reviews, photos and missing toilets. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@topitsolutions.co.nz.

Using Peepals as a guest

You can browse the map, filter toilets and get walking directions without an account. We don't collect any personal information from guests.

Your location

  • With your permission, the app reads your location while it's open to show toilets within about 2 km. It never tracks you in the background.
  • To find toilets, the app sends your approximate position to public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers. That request isn't linked to your account, and we don't store your location.
  • The map itself is drawn by Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps (Android), which see your position to show the blue dot, under their own privacy policies.
  • Walking directions open in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

If you become a member

Signing in with your email lets you post reviews, photos and missing toilets. We then store:

WhatWhyWho can see it
Email addressTo send your sign-in code and identify your accountOnly us
Nickname (e.g. "Peepal 4F2A")Shown on your reviewsEveryone
Reviews and tips (ratings and short text)To help other peopleEveryone
Photos you uploadTo show what a toilet is likeEveryone
Toilets you add, including their location (where you were standing when you added them)To put missing toilets on the mapEveryone (shown without your nickname)
Reports you send about other contentTo remove offensive or wrong contentOnly us

Please don't include people, faces or private details in photos or reviews.

Where it's stored

Member data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, and is only used to run Peepals. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools. Some data, like reported content you hide and a copy of the last toilet list, is kept only on your phone.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the Profile tab and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, email, nickname, reviews, photos, reports and the toilets you added. You can also ask us to delete it by email. We keep your data only until you delete your account.

Children

Peepals isn't directed at children under 13, and children under 13 shouldn't create an account.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing support@topitsolutions.co.nz. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
