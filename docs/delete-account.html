Delete your Peepals account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Peepals and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

By email (no app needed)

Email support@topitsolutions.co.nz from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Peepals account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Everything linked to your account, permanently: your email address, nickname, reviews and tips, photos, reports you sent, and toilets you added. Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from what's still in our hosting provider's backups, which are overwritten within 7 days.
